# B0aty HCIM Guide V2 (OSRS Wiki)
Source: https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Guide:B0aty_HCIM_Guide_V2

Guide by B0aty (originally created by Tpapa). Route to Song of the Elves completion with 70 Herblore brickwall. Also completes Recipe for Disaster, Moons of Peril, Royal Titans, and optionally Piety.

**All lamps go to Herblore.**

---

## Guide Structure

- Episode 1: Banks 1-24 (Tutorial Island through early questing)
- Episode 2: Banks 24-75 (Mid-early questing, skills training)
- Episode 3: Banks 76-104B (Agility, more quests, Shilo Village area)
- Episode 4: Banks 105-150 (Mid-game, further questing)
- Episode 5: Banks 151-175 (Legend's Quest, RFD, Barrows area)
- Episode 6: Bank 176 through Combat Achievements
- Episode 7: Combat Training/Questing through Barrows
- Episode 8: Herblore through Perilous Moons
- Episode 9: Bank 177 through Wrapping up (Song of the Elves)

## Bosses Killed / Mentioned

### Early/Mid-game
- Bandit Champion (Demon Slayer)
- Hespori (Farming Guild, plant as early as possible) - Note: VERY dangerous early; get CAs only
- Scurrius - Kill on Defensive for 65 Attack/Strength/680k Defence XP; Combat Achievements
- Cave Horrors - Until Black Mask drop (Slayer)
- Armoured Zombies - For Zombie Axe drop (Ice Burst/Burst method; buy Ice Sacks from LMS or buy runes)
- Giant Mole - Combat Achievement (No Damage kill)
- Dagannoth (from Horror from the Deep quest)
- Dagannoth Rex (Iban Blast method; Berserker Ring)

### Mid-game
- Barrows Brothers (Ahrim, Dharok, Guthan, Karil, Torag, Verac) - Tank gear first, then full sets
  - Max hits: Ahrim 20, Dharok 29 (57 at 1hp), Guthan 24, Karil 20, Torag 23, Verac 23
  - Weak to Wind Spells
  - Build Barrows Portal at 78 Magic (boost to 83 with Red Evil Dave Stew)
- Royal Titans - Until both Prayer Scrolls (Deadeye + Mystic Vigour)
- Chambers of Xeric - Scout early; do CAs:
  - No Falling Rocks (Guardians)
  - Long Range safespot a Shaman
  - Ice Demon with no prayer/damage/fire spells
  - Tightrope Skip
- Entry Theatre of Blood (TOB) - Some easy CAs

### Late-game (Post-Song of the Elves / V3 Guide)
- Corrupted Gauntlet - For Enhanced Weapon Seed (Bow of Faerdhinen) with Tier 3 Preparation
- Demonic Gorillas - 2 Zenytes (1st Anguish, 2nd Suffering)
- Zulrah - Bowfa camp until Serp helm (use Cure Me Lunar spell)
- Theatre of Blood (full)
- Inferno (Infernal Cape)

## Key Milestones in Order

1. Complete early quests for Waterfall XP (melee to adamant scimitar level)
2. Train Agility to 70 (Song of the Elves requirement) via Agility Pyramid for 1M+ GP
3. 60 Marks of Grace -> Graceful Legs (Rogues' Den)
4. Complete Rogues' Den for full Rogue outfit (doubles pickpocket loot)
5. Complete Recipe for Disaster -> Barrows Gloves + Rune Gloves
6. Get Dragon Scimitar at Ape Atoll (after Monkey Madness)
7. Dragon Defender from Warriors' Guild
8. Burn Slayer to ~58 (with Konar at 75 combat)
9. Train to 65 Attack / 65 Strength
10. Barrows for tank gear (helm, platebody, platelegs)
11. Kill Rex for Berserker Ring (optional: Iban Blast 70 Magic)
12. Kill Cave Horrors for Black Mask -> Slayer Helmet
13. Kill Armoured Zombies for Zombie Axe
14. 78 Magic -> boost to 83 for Barrows Portal in POH
15. Complete Legend's Quest
16. Desert Treasure -> Ancient Staff (80,000 GP from Eblis)
17. Complete all Recipe for Disaster subquests -> Barrows Gloves
18. Song of the Elves (requires 70 Herblore, 70 Agility, many quests)
19. Royal Titans -> Prayer Scrolls
20. Corrupted Gauntlet -> Enhanced Weapon Seed (Bow of Faerdhinen)
21. 83 Construction (Mahogany Homes) -> full POH setup
22. Inferno -> Infernal Cape
23. Demonic Gorillas -> Zenytes
24. Zulrah -> Bowfa camp
25. Theatre of Blood
