## Overview / Game Phases

### Early-game Milestones
- Rune crossbow from Crazy archaeologist
- Broad bolts from broader fletching
- Black dragonhide armour from hard clues
- Fire cape from TzHaar Fight Cave
- Fighter torso from Barbarian Assault
- Barrows gloves from Recipe for Disaster
- Dragon defender from Warriors' Guild
- Iban's staff from Underground Pass
- Imbued god cape from Mage Arena II
- Berserker ring (i) from Dagannoth Rex
- Zombie axe from Armoured zombies
- Black mask (i) and Slayer helmet (i) from cave horrors

### Mid-game Key Upgrades
- Barrows and Moons armour sets and weaponry
- Elite Void Knight equipment from Pest Control
- Deadeye, Mystic vigour prayer scrolls, and Twinflame staff from Royal Titans
- Amulet of fury (90 Crafting, 87 Magic to enchant; 85 Crafting with boost)
- Tormented synapses (Scorching bow, Emberlight, Purging staff) and Burning claws from Tormented Demons
- Dragon boots from Spiritual mages
- Abyssal whip from Abyssal demons
- Trident of the seas from Cave kraken
- Dragon warhammer from Lizardman shamans
- Basilisk jaw from Basilisk Knights
- Bow of Faerdhinen from The Corrupted Gauntlet
- Saradomin brews with 81 Herblore

### Late-game Highlights
- Zenyte jewellery from Demonic gorillas
- Toxic blowpipe and Trident of the swamp from Zulrah
- God Wars Dungeon bosses:
  - K'ril Tsutsaroth: Zamorakian spear, Staff of the dead
  - General Graardor: Bandos armour, Bandos godsword
  - Commander Zilyana: Armadyl crossbow, Saradomin godsword
- Primordial boots from Cerberus
- Occult necklace from Smoke devils
- Amulet of rancour and Noxious halberd from Araxxor
- Dragon hunter lance and Ferocious gloves from Alchemical Hydra
- Infernal cape from the Inferno

## Key Skills Notes

- Agility: Train early; graceful outfit (260 marks of grace at 60+ Agility); run energy restoration
- Slayer: Core of mid-game; unlocks Abyssal whip (85), Trident (87), Alchemical Hydra (95)
- Herblore: 81 for Saradomin brews; 77 for Stamina potions
- Construction: 78 for Barrows Teleport portal (with +5 boost from red spicy stew = 83)
- Crafting: 85 for Amulet of fury (with boost); 80 without boost
- Magic: 87 for Amulet of fury enchant

## Money-making Notes

- West Ardougne General Store: buys at 55% value (vs 40% elsewhere)
- Martin Thwait's Lost and Found: pays full High Alchemy value
- High Level Alchemy (55 Magic): best passive GP method in mid/late game
- Blackjacking (45 Thieving): good early GP
- Stealing valuables (50 Thieving): good passive GP + prayer XP from blessed bone statuettes
- Agility Pyramid (30+ Agility, recommended 60+): 10,000 coins per artefact
